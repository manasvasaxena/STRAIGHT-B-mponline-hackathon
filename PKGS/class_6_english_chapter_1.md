# Class 6 English - Chapter 1
## A Tale of Two Birds

### 1. Chapter Summary
A big storm blew down the tree where a mother bird lived with her two newborn chicks. The mother bird was killed, but the two young birds were blown away to different sides of the forest. One landed near a cave of robbers, while the other landed outside a quiet rishi's ashram.

### 2. Key Takeaways
- **Environment Shapes Behavior**: A person or animal is known by the company they keep.
- The bird near the robbers learned to speak harshly like robbers.
- The bird near the ashram learned to welcome guests politely and gently.

### 3. Practice Questions
1. How did the two birds get separated?
   *Answer*: A strong storm knocked down their nest tree and blew them to different parts of the forest.
2. What did the first bird say to the king?
   *Answer*: "Quick, hurry up! There is someone under the tree. Come and take his jewels and his horse!"
